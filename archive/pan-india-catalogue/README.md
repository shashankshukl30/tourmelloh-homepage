# Archived: pan-India luxury catalogue (October 2026)

The first Tourmelloh build covered all of India: 34 journeys, 29 experiences, 13 regions,
31 city guides, 6 collections and 12 journal articles. The client's developer brief (v5,
31 October 2026) narrowed the business to Varanasi and the surrounding pilgrimage region,
so this catalogue is kept here for reference and never deployed.

- Excluded from Vercel by `.vercelignore` and from the TypeScript build by `tsconfig.json`.
- The exact state of the live site before the change is tagged `pan-india-catalogue-2026-10-31`.
- Photographs it used remain in `public/images/library` (shared with the new catalogue).
