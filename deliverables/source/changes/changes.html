What changed
What we changed

Built around how your
guests choose and enquire.

20 packages, one structure

Every package from your document, with its day-by-day plan, overnights and gateways, and the Standard, Premium and Luxury lines for each.

Tiers you can compare

Three cards per package. Every upgrade is marked against the level below, plus a side-by-side view. A new Tiers page compares the levels in general.

WhatsApp that names the package

Every “Get a quote” opens WhatsApp with the package code, name and tier already written, so you know exactly what the guest is asking about.

My Journey, like a cart

Guests add a package, services and experiences, or single upgrades from a higher tier (a wheelchair, a photographer), then ask for one quotation.

Homepage, packages first

Packages straight after the finder, grouped as Kashi & pilgrim routes, Buddhist circuit and Pind daan. About and tiers moved to the end. A team section added.

Package cards that sell

Name on the photograph, nights, days and places in bold, and two clear buttons: Get your itinerary and Get a quote.

A hero film of your places

Taj Mahal, Lucknow and Jaipur removed. The film now shows Varanasi, Prayagraj, Ayodhya and Bodh Gaya, mostly real footage.

Photos that are true to place

Replaced a Ladakh mountain road, a Jammu station sign, the Taj behind a wheelchair, a hill-station train, and weak aarti, silk, food and music shots.

Specialist collections

Her Own Way (women, female host), SHRAVAN (parents), Together (families), the Buddhist circuit and Wellness, plus Destination Weddings with venues.

Found on Google, found on the site

Site search that understands guests’ words (Banaras, pind daan, private taxi), a full sitemap, structured data, and every old link redirected.

Menus and wording

Menu now reads Packages, Services, Experiences, Destination Weddings. Titles corrected for commas and capitals across the site.

Letterhead and signature

Brand letterhead (PDF and Word) and email signature, now with +91 63910 85555.
