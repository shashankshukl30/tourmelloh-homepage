# Tourmelloh website: scope notes and inputs needed

Prepared by Digiocular for Tourmelloh · 31 October 2026 · Reference: Developer Brief v5

This note confirms what the website build includes beyond the brief's boundaries, and the few items only Tourmelloh can provide. Everything else in the brief is being built.

## 1. Not included in this phase

These items fall outside the agreed scope of work. Each can be quoted separately later.

| Item | What this means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Admin panel / CMS and staff login** | Staff will not edit the website themselves. Content updates (journeys, prices, dates, reviews) are sent to Digiocular and published by us. |
| **Enquiry database and dashboard** | Enquiries are not stored in an online database with filtering or CSV export. Each enquiry carries a reference number and its full selection, delivered by email to the Tourmelloh inbox; the guest keeps a printable copy. |
| **Reviews management tool** | Reviews and testimonials are added by us once Tourmelloh supplies them with the guest's consent. |
| **Live group-departure tracking** | Women's departures show a status we set on request (Dates coming soon, Open for enquiries, Departure confirmed, Full). Seat counts and quorum are not tracked automatically. |
| **Payments, quotation editor, CRM** | As stated in the brief, the website ends at enquiry capture. Quotations and payments are handled by the Tourmelloh team directly. |
| **Invented content** | We do not create reviews, testimonials, departure dates, prices or venue partnerships. Where these are missing, the section stays hidden or carries an honest "on request" label. |
